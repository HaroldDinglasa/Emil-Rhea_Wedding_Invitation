Our journey together

Every love story has a beginning, and ours started in the most unexpected place—college.

I was a student assistant in the Criminology Department, even though I was taking up Business Administration. It was there that our paths crossed. What began as a simple friendship grew even closer when he became part of our circle, the CrimBabies.

From shared conversations and laughter to unforgettable college memories, our friendship slowly blossomed into something more. As the years passed, our love was strengthened by trust, patience, and unwavering commitment. We celebrated life’s happiest moments together and stood by each other through every challenge, even when distance became part of our journey.

For eight wonderful years, we have chosen each other every single day. Through God’s grace and perfect timing, He has led us to this beautiful moment.

Now, with grateful hearts and joyful anticipation, we are ready to begin our greatest adventure yet—as husband and wife.

Thank you for being part of our story. Your love, prayers, and presence make our celebration even more meaningful. We can’t wait to celebrate this special day with you as we begin our forever.

With love,
Emil & Rhea
